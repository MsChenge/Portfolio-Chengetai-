About Me

Hi! I'm Chengetai Shereni (also known as Miss Cheng), a writer and self-taught builder living in Yokohama, Japan. I create Japanese learning quizzes for foreigners living in Japan, write stories, and design workbooks — including "My Next Season", a 12-week goal workbook. My current goal is to help learners master 1,500 Japanese words by January 2027.

Education & Qualifications

  • Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL) — ESL Language Instructor — TEFL South Africa, 2018–2019
  • Certified in Cybersecurity — ISC2 (International Information System Security Certification Consortium)
